Welcome!

I’m Ethan Pichon, a Mechanical Engineering student at Caltech (Robotics minor, 4.0 GPA) focused on robotics, autonomy, and mechanical design. I’m driven by a curiosity for how systems work, and how thoughtful engineering can make them work better. In particular, I’m interested in pursuing precise control for dynamic and complex environments such as space, subsea, and surgical robotics.

I’ve applied that across research and hands-on builds: developing ROS2 motion-planning for a human-space colony rover at the UMD Space Systems Lab, benchmarking reinforcement-learning algorithms (PPO, MPPI, CEM) on space-manipulation tasks as a Herzog SURF Fellow, and teaching robotics as a Caltech TA, where my LiDAR research improved obstacle-avoidance accuracy by 65%.

In the shop, I’ve machined and assembled a 40 lb robot capable of scaling a 37° incline, written C++ PID controllers for full autonomy, and derived the inverse kinematics that make my robots move precisely. Whether I’m designing hardware, modeling dynamics, machining parts, or integrating electronics and software, I love bringing complex ideas into reality.

This site is a space to share the robotics projects, research, and mechanical builds that have shaped my journey so far. Thanks for visiting, and I hope you enjoy exploring my work.
